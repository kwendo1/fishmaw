About Mawke Welding

Quality steel solutions for every space

Craftsmanship Meets Modern Technology

Mawke Welding and Fabrications is a leading manufacturer of high-quality steel products. We specialize in the design, fabrication, and installation of gates, doors, windows, railings, and security grills for residential, commercial, and industrial properties.

We combine skilled craftsmanship, premium materials, and modern technology to deliver products that are strong, durable, and elegant.
